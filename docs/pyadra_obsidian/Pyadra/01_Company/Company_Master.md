## 1. WHAT IS PYADRA

Pyadra is a platform where anyone can discover,
participate in, invest in, and acquire digital projects.

If you want to be part of the tech world without
being a developer — Pyadra is your entry point.

If you have a digital project and need visibility,
investment, or buyers — Pyadra is your stage.

**Tagline:** Lo que dejas importa.
**In English:** What you leave behind matters.

---

## 2. WHY IT EXISTS

Technology is advancing fast. Most people feel
left out — they have money, ideas, or talent
but no clear way to participate.

Pyadra exists to change that.
Every person is a pawn that can become a queen.
The path is Pyadra.

**Name origin:**
Pyada (pawn in Hindi) + RA (path in Latin) = PYADRA
Born from a chess game and a moment of clarity.

---

## 3. WHO IT'S FOR

**The accessible investor**
Has some disposable income. Sees tech advancing
and wants to be part of it without needing to be
a developer. Wants something tangible, low-cost,
and meaningful. Wants to feel like they're
investing in something real.

**The creator / new talent**
Has a digital project or idea. Needs visibility,
a platform, and people who believe in it.
Wants to monetize without building everything alone.

---

## 4. THE THREE TYPES OF PROJECTS

Every project on Pyadra fits one of three models:

**Type 1 — Native Project**
Lives completely inside Pyadra.
Logic, payments, and experience all within Pyadra.
Example: EterniCapsule.

**Type 2 — External Project with Internal Product**
The project lives outside Pyadra (YouTube, Spotify,
Shopify, own website). But it has an exclusive
product that can only be acquired through Pyadra.
Example: Orbit 77 — podcast lives on YouTube/Spotify,
but the membership credential is only sold on Pyadra.

**Type 3 — Exhibited External Project**
Displayed on Pyadra with its dashboard.
The link takes users to the external platform.
Example: Figurines → links to external store.

**Key principle:**
Every project must be able to exist and operate
independently of Pyadra. Pyadra is the stage.
The project is the actor. The actor can leave
and perform elsewhere at any time.

## 6. PROJECT DASHBOARD

Every project in Galaxy has a public dashboard
visible before entering. It shows:

- What the project is (2 lines max)
- Main product and its price
- Total project value
- Available participation for acquisition
- Participation already sold
- What a buyer receives
- Link or direct access to the project

This dashboard is the core of Pyadra's
investment experience. All data pulls live
from the project's real metrics.
No hardcoded numbers anywhere.

## 7. BUSINESS MODEL

### Phase 1 — Products (current)
Users buy individual products inside each project.
Each project defines its own pricing.
Pyadra facilitates the transaction.

### Phase 2 — Project Acquisition (next)
- Creators publish projects with acquisition terms
- Buyers can acquire full projects or a percentage
- Original creator retains minority stake
  and perpetual royalties
- Pyadra takes a transaction fee
- Legal review required before launch (ASIC Australia)

### Phase 3 — Token Economy (future)
- Users buy Pyadra native tokens first
- Tokens are used to acquire project stakes
- Removes the psychological friction of real money
- Requires full legal review (ASIC Australia)
  before any implementation
- Start with internal Pyadra Credits as bridge

**Note on legal:**
Selling project stakes may be classified as
a financial security by ASIC (Australia).
Legal counsel required before Phase 2 launch.
Token model requires separate regulatory review.
