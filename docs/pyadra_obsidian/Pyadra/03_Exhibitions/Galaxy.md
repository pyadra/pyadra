## WHAT GALAXY IS

Galaxy is the first active exhibition inside Pyadra. It is an investment showcase first, a product store second.

The product inside each project is proof that something real exists and works today. But what Galaxy primarily offers is the projects themselves — as assets that can be acquired.

Every project in Galaxy answers two questions at once:

- **What can I experience today?** — the product, the price, how to enter
- **What can I own?** — the project as an asset, its value, how to acquire it

Galaxy is **60% investment showcase, 40% product store.**

Every person who arrives at Galaxy can:

1. Enter the ritual of a project — use the product, live the experience
2. Buy the project — become a full or partial owner

Both options are visible on every project. No hidden information. No confusing phases. Everything clear from the first moment.

**Who Galaxy speaks to:** Curious people, not professional investors. Normal people who can see value in a digital project and want to be part of something real from early on.

## BUSINESS MODEL — HOW ACQUISITION WORKS

### The Pyadra model

Pyadra does not sell projects and disappear. Pyadra is the infrastructure where projects live — like an intelligent hosting platform that participates in the revenue.

When someone buys a project:

- The buyer owns the project
- The project can stay on Pyadra (recommended) or the buyer can take it elsewhere
- Pyadra retains a small percentage of the revenue generated — not of the project value, but of what it produces each month
- That percentage covers hosting, infrastructure, and operation
- The buyer operates the project, grows it, sells — and Pyadra receives its share automatically

### Why keeping it on Pyadra makes sense

- The buyer does not need to worry about infrastructure
- The project stays visible in Galaxy — it has an audience and an ecosystem
- Pyadra maintains the technical integration
- It is simpler than taking it elsewhere and operating it from scratch

### Participation models — in plain language

|Model|What it means|
|---|---|
|I take it completely|The buyer acquires 100% and operates it outside Pyadra. Pyadra charges once and no longer participates.|
|We are partners|The buyer acquires a percentage. Pyadra retains the rest. Revenue is split proportionally.|
|I own it, Pyadra runs it|The buyer is the owner but the project lives on Pyadra. Pyadra retains a % of revenue as an operational fee.|

### What Pyadra always retains per project

|Project|Pyadra fee|Basis|
|---|---|---|
|EterniCapsule|$1 of every $9 AUD generated (~11%)|Per capsule sold|
|Orbit 77|To be defined — community model|Per credential sold|
|Figurines|To be defined — physical model|Per figurine sold|
|Ebooks|To be defined — editorial model|Per book sold|

_Note: Exact fees for Orbit 77, Figurines, and Ebooks are defined before Phase 2 launch._

### What happens after someone expresses interest

There is no automatic checkout. Acquiring a project is an important decision — it happens through conversation.

When someone clicks "Buy the project":

1. They see the complete project information
2. They express their interest with name and email
3. The CEO of Pyadra contacts them personally within 48 hours
4. The negotiation happens between people, not between forms

## INFORMATION EACH PROJECT MUST SHOW

This defines what information is required for every project in Galaxy. It does not define how it is displayed — that is the designer's decision. It defines what is mandatory and the order of importance.

### 1. Identity

- Project name
- Project type (Podcast, Digital Vault, Physical Product, Publishing)
- Project logo (image path, or fallback to gem icon)
- Founders (who created it)
- One line explaining what it is — no jargon, no technical language

### 2. Traction — what already exists

What proves the project is real and working today. Structure: array of `{label, value}` pairs.

**Current metrics per project:**
- **Orbit 77:** S2 Funding ($340/$1,000), Supporters (12)
- **EterniCapsule:** Capsules sealed (Early Stage), System status (Operational)

Uses **"Active vs Potential"** pattern — no exaggerated projections, just honest current state + future opportunities.

**Structure:** `{value, description}`

## ACTIONS AND BUTTONS

### "Enter the project →"

- **Text:** "Enter the project →" (was "Enter the ritual →")
- **Action:** Navigates to the project's URL using `window.location.href`
- **URLs by project:**
  - Orbit 77: `/exhibitions/galaxy/orbit`
  - EterniCapsule: `/exhibitions/galaxy/ethernicapsule`
  - Figurines: `/exhibitions/galaxy/figurines`
  - Ebooks: `/exhibitions/galaxy/ebooks`
- **Tone:** Invitation, experience, accessible
- **Visual:** Emerald fill `#059669`, white text
- **State:** Always active — the product exists today
- **Narrative:** This is the light door. Anyone can enter.

### "Express interest →"

- **Text:** "Express interest →" (was "Buy the project →")
- **Action:** Opens the Express Interest form (replaces panel content)
- **Tone:** Decision, investment, serious
- **Visual:** Dark background `#0A120E`, emerald text `#059669`
- **State:** Active — acquisition is possible today via conversation
- **Form structure:**
  1. Three acquisition model options (radio buttons):
     - "We become partners" — Partial acquisition, revenue split
     - "I own it, you run it" — Full ownership, Pyadra operates
     - "I take it completely" — 100% acquisition, external operation
  2. Input fields: Name (required), Email (required), Message (optional)
  3. Submit sends to `/api/contact` with type "express-interest"
  4. Email notification to eduardo@pyadra.io
- **Confirmation:** "Eduardo will contact you personally within 48 hours"
- **Narrative:** This is the dark door. Expressing interest means stepping toward ownership.

**The narrative logic:** Entering the project is the light door — open to everyone. Expressing interest is the dark door — for those ready to own something. Both doors are always visible. The contrast between them communicates the difference without a single word of explanation.
